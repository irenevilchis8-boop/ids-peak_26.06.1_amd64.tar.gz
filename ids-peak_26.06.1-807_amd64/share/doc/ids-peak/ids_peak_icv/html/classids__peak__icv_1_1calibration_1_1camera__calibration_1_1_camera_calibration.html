Detailed Description

Class for calibrating a camera.

The CameraCalibration calculates the intrinsic and extrinsic parameters, as well as the mean reprojection error.

◆ process()

CalibrationResult process ( self,
ImageArray images )

Applies the camera calibration and calculates the intrinsic and extrinsic camera parameters based on several views of a calibration pattern.

Furthermore, it computes the mean reprojection error.

Note
This operation disregards any specified image regions. It processes the entire image.
Parameters
imagesA list of images containing a calibration plate in different poses.
Exceptions
MismatchExceptionIf no images are provided, or if the sizes of the images are different.
NotSupportedExceptionIf the pattern of the calibration plate is not supported or the image has an unsupported format.
TargetNotFoundExceptionIf the marker points were not found on the given image.

ids_peak_icv 1.1
