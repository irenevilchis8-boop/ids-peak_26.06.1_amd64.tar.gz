Detailed Description

template<typename PointType>
class peak::icv::detail::Polygon< PointType >

A polygon is a two-dimensional geometric shape that is made up of a finite number of straight line segments connected end-to-end to form an open or closed figure. A polygon is represented by the points where the line segments meet.
