Detailed Description

Sharpening is an image pipeline module that applies a sharpening filter to enhance image detail.

It enhances edges and fine structures by emphasizing high-frequency components in the image.

The strength of the effect is controlled by the sharpness level, where 0 results in no change.

Warning
Increasing the sharpness level can also amplify image noise, especially at higher values. Excessive sharpening may introduce artifacts such as halos around edges.

Defaults
  • level = 0
