Detailed Description

Compresses the dynamic range of HDR images using a logarithmic curve adapted to human visual perception.

It is based on the Adaptive Logarithmic Mapping For Displaying High Contrast Scenes Paper from F. Drago, K. Myszkowski, T. Annen and N. Chiba from the year 2003.
